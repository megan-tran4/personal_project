Context

We wanted to think about a way to develop a more social form of Spotify for users ages 18-25.

1. Research

We conducted interviews with Spotify users to see what they use Spotify for. Through this, we found the following insights:

  1. Users typically share music on external social media apps rather than through their music apps.

  2. Users listen to music most hours of the day in order to release stress.

  3. Users don’t feel like their algorithmically-suggested music is adequate.
