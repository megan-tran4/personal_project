Context

We wanted to think about a way to develop a more social form of Spotify for users ages 18-25.

1. Research

We conducted interviews with Spotify users to see what they use Spotify for. Through this, we found the following insights:

Using these insights, we created the following HMW questions:

  1. HMW make sharing music within the listening experience more seamless and social?

  2. HMW help users express their music taste and discoveries without leaving the platform?

  3. HMW help users guide their own music recommendation experience?

  4. HMW help users find community through music that reflects their current mood/goals?
